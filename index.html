Email Finder: Privacy Policy

Effective date: 11 November 2026. Publisher: TeraVios, operated by TeraInvoice LLC ("we"). Contact: terainvoiceapp@gmail.com.

What the extension does

Email Finder is a Chrome extension. When you click its icon on a website, it reads that page, and that same website's Contact and About pages, to find public email addresses and links to the site's Facebook, Instagram and LinkedIn pages, and shows them to you.

Data the extension handles

  • Website content of the page you click on (and that site's contact pages): processed inside your browser to find emails and social links. It is not sent to us.
  • Other pages of the same website: when you press "Scan whole site" (or open the contact pages), the extension requests that website's own pages, listed in its robots.txt, sitemap and links, from inside your browser tab and reads them locally. Their content is not sent to us.
  • Leads you choose to save (website, page title, emails found, social links, date): stored only in your browser's local extension storage on your device. We cannot see them. You can remove them any time with "×" or "Clear all" in My list, or by removing the extension.
  • Usage counter (which websites you scanned today, used to apply the free daily limit) and a note that you accepted the consent screen: stored locally on your device.

Data sent to other services

  • Google Public DNS (dns.google): for each email found, the extension sends only the email's domain name (for example example.com, never the full address) to check that the domain can receive mail. Google's privacy policy applies to that request.
  • ExtensionPay and Stripe (payments): to manage subscriptions the extension communicates with ExtensionPay (extensionpay.com), which receives a random installation identifier and whether you have paid. If you upgrade, your payment email and payment details are collected by ExtensionPay and Stripe, not by us, and their policies apply. We do not receive your card details.

What we do not do

  • We do not collect, store or receive the pages you scan or the emails found.
  • We do not sell or transfer user data to third parties, other than as described above and as needed to provide the extension's single purpose, to comply with law, or for security.
  • We do not use or transfer data for advertising, personalised or interest-based ads, or credit and lending decisions.
  • Humans do not read your data.

Limited Use

The use of information received from web pages complies with the Chrome Web Store User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Data is used only to provide the extension's single, user-facing purpose: finding contact emails and social links on the website you choose.

Security

All network requests made by the extension use HTTPS. Saved leads stay on your device inside the browser's extension storage.

Your responsibilities

Email addresses you collect are personal data in many countries. You are responsible for using them lawfully (for example GDPR, UK PECR, CAN-SPAM), including honouring opt-outs.
